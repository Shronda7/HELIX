The Foundation

Why Mathematics?

Traditional frameworks treat trauma, addiction, and healing as sequential events. Clinical experience tells a more complex story: these states are simultaneous and interdependent.

By modeling them as coupled differential equations—the same mathematical language used to describe planetary orbits, population dynamics, and fluid mechanics—HELIX makes hidden trajectories visible. What is visible can be understood. What is understood can be influenced.

The double helix is not a metaphor borrowed from biology. It is a structural truth. Trauma and healing do not alternate—they co-evolve. They are bound to each other the same way the two strands of DNA are bound: opposing in direction, unified in function.

The goal is not to make people into equations.
The goal is to make the equations feel like people.
α

Coupled Dynamics

Every function influences every other. Nothing in the model is isolated—just as nothing in human experience is isolated.

∫

Non-Linear Interaction

Trauma impairs regulation; impaired regulation reduces healing; reduced healing allows trauma to persist. The system spirals—in either direction.

Δt

Time-Dependent Evolution

These are trajectories, not snapshots. The model shows how states evolve, not just where they start.

∂

Intervention Modeling

Adjusting support levels and healing rates produces measurable changes across every function. Therapeutic leverage becomes visible.

Core Mathematics

The Five Functions

T(t)

Trauma Intensity

Decays exponentially under healing conditions, but experiences cyclic resurgence through triggers. Never simply disappears.

dT/dt = −α·T·H + β·sin(ωt)
A(t)

Addiction State

Self-reinforcing loop with tolerance buildup. Models how compulsive patterns resist disruption from the inside.

dA/dt = γ·A·(1−A/Amax) − δ·E
E(t)

Emotional Regulation

Directly affected by trauma load and available support. The system’s ability to manage its own inputs.

dE/dt = ε·H − ζ·T − η·A
H(t)

Healing Trajectory

An integration over time of support systems and self-regulation capacity. Healing is cumulative—it compounds.

dH/dt = θ·(S − H) + ι·E
R(t)

Dynamic Resilience

Grows or diminishes based on the current state of all other functions. Not a trait—a system output.

dR/dt = κ·(E + H − T − A)

A Note on Scope

HELIX is a conceptual modeling tool, not a diagnostic or clinical instrument. It does not predict individual outcomes or replace professional assessment.

  • Parameters are illustrative, chosen to produce meaningful behavior
  • Human experience is irreducibly complex—no model captures it fully
  • HELIX is designed to open conversations, not close them

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline or the SAMHSA National Helpline.
